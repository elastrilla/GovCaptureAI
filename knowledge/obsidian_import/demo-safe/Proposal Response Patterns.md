# Proposal Response Patterns

GovCaptureAI proposal drafts should be structured as a starting point for human review, not a final submission.

## Common Technical Response Structure

1. Executive summary
2. Understanding of the requirement
3. Technical approach
4. Management approach
5. Staffing or key personnel approach
6. Relevant past performance
7. Risk management
8. Transition or implementation plan
9. Compliance and quality controls

## Drafting Principles

- Start from the opportunity's actual requirements.
- Use company capabilities and evidence to support claims.
- Avoid generic language when specific evidence is available.
- Highlight measurable outcomes and delivery controls.
- Clearly flag gaps that need human review.
- Keep the tone credible, direct, and buyer-focused.

## Demo-Safe Message

GovCaptureAI accelerates the first draft, but the contractor remains responsible for final review, pricing, compliance, and submission decisions.
