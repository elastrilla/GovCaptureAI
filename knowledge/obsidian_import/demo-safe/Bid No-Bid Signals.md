# Bid No-Bid Signals

GovCaptureAI should help small businesses decide whether an opportunity deserves pursuit time.

## Strong Fit Signals

- The opportunity aligns with the company's core capabilities.
- The NAICS code matches the company's target market.
- The agency is already a target or past customer.
- The set-aside type matches the company's certifications or socioeconomic status.
- The due date allows enough time for a credible response.
- The company has relevant past performance or reusable evidence.
- The scope is focused enough for a small business team to execute.

## Caution Signals

- The scope is broad but the company has limited evidence.
- The due date is too close for a quality response.
- The opportunity appears wired for an incumbent.
- Required certifications are missing.
- The company would need too many subcontractors to respond credibly.
- The solicitation requires pricing, compliance, or security posture the company cannot support yet.

## Recommended Product Behavior

Qualification should produce a clear score, recommendation, and rationale. The rationale should explain both strengths and gaps so the user can make a human bid/no-bid decision.
