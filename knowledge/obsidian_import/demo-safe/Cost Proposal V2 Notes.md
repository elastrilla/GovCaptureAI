# Cost Proposal V2 Notes

Cost proposal support is planned for a future GovCaptureAI version because pricing requires careful controls and company-specific inputs.

## Planned Cost Proposal Areas

- Labor categories
- FTE estimates
- Period of performance
- Option years
- Travel assumptions
- Other direct costs
- Subcontractor inputs
- Pricing assumptions
- Basis of estimate narrative
- Exportable cost workbook

## Guardrail

GovCaptureAI should assist with cost proposal organization and basis-of-estimate drafting. It should not present itself as an automatic pricing authority.

## Customer-Facing Positioning

The current product focuses on opportunity discovery, qualification, evidence readiness, workflow tracking, and technical proposal drafting. Cost proposal support is planned as a controlled Version 2 module.
