# GovCaptureAI Buyer FAQ

## What is GovCaptureAI?

GovCaptureAI is an AI-assisted capture management platform that helps contractors discover federal opportunities, qualify fit, track workflow status, and generate draft proposal content.

## Is this only a SAM.gov search tool?

No. SAM.gov data is the foundation, but the product goal is the full capture workflow: discovery, qualification, bid/no-bid support, and proposal readiness.

## Who is it for?

The first target users are small businesses, business development managers, capture managers, proposal managers, proposal consultants, and executives who need a clearer view of pipeline quality.

## How does qualification work?

GovCaptureAI compares opportunity details against company profile information such as capabilities, certifications, target NAICS codes, target agencies, differentiators, and past performance. It returns a score, recommendation, and rationale that a human can review.

## Does the AI make the bid/no-bid decision?

No. The AI supports the decision by organizing evidence and surfacing fit or risk. The contractor remains responsible for the final decision.

## What notice types can it review?

The current workflow supports RFI, RFQ, RFP, and Sources Sought filtering so teams can separate market research, quote requests, proposal opportunities, and early pipeline signals.

## Can it generate a proposal?

The current proof of concept can generate an initial draft response using opportunity details, company profile data, qualification rationale, and company document inputs. The output is a starting point for review, not a final submission.

## What makes this useful for small businesses?

Small teams often lack dedicated capture operations support. GovCaptureAI helps them create a repeatable review process without requiring a large enterprise capture system.

## What data does the contractor need to provide?

At minimum, a useful pilot needs company capabilities, certifications, target NAICS codes, target agencies, differentiators, and past performance examples.

## What should a pilot prove?

A pilot should prove that the team can screen opportunities faster, explain bid/no-bid choices more clearly, and produce a stronger first draft when an opportunity is worth pursuing.

## What is not included yet?

The proof of concept does not yet include full authentication, team collaboration, CRM integration, automated compliance review, final proposal formatting, or automated submission.

## What is the next product step?

The next safest product step after the sales package is proposal export support, so generated drafts can move from the dashboard into a shareable document format.
