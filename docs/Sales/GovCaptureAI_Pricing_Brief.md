# GovCaptureAI Pricing Brief

## Pricing Position

GovCaptureAI should be sold first as a consulting-led pilot, not as a fully packaged SaaS subscription. The product is still early, and the strongest buyer promise is proving whether AI-assisted capture workflow can reduce review time, improve bid/no-bid confidence, and accelerate first-draft proposal content.

## Recommended Offer Ladder

| Offer | Price | Best For | Outcome |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Discovery Sprint | $4,500 | Buyer wants to validate fit before committing to a pilot. | Clear pilot plan and target-market definition. |
| Focused Pilot | $15,000 | Buyer has a target market and wants proof on real opportunities. | Working capture workflow on up to 25 opportunities. |
| Implementation Setup | $36,000 | Buyer wants to operationalize the workflow for a small team. | Team-ready setup, training, and workflow handoff. |

## Lead Offer

Lead with the Focused Pilot.

This package best matches the current product story:

- one company profile,
- one target agency, NAICS, or market lane,
- up to 25 real opportunities,
- qualification scoring and rationale,
- workflow status review,
- proposal draft and export demo,
- buyer readout with lessons learned.

## When To Use Each Package

Discovery Sprint:
Use when the buyer likes the idea but has not yet chosen a target market or provided enough company context.

Focused Pilot:
Use as the primary sales motion. It gives the buyer proof on real opportunities without making GovCaptureAI sound like a mature enterprise platform too early.

Implementation Setup:
Use after a successful pilot when the buyer wants training, repeatable setup, and broader workflow adoption.

## Scope Boundaries

The early packages should not promise:

- full enterprise CRM replacement,
- automated bid submission,
- legal or compliance review,
- production-grade multi-user permissions,
- custom integrations,
- guaranteed proposal win outcomes.

## Suggested Talk Track

"The best way to evaluate GovCaptureAI is not a long procurement cycle. It is a focused pilot using your company profile and a real target market. We will run a set of opportunities through the workflow, document bid/no-bid rationale, generate a proposal draft, and decide whether the approach saves enough time and improves enough decision quality to justify broader adoption."

## Pricing Notes

The pricing model is intentionally editable. Package prices, delivery hours, internal delivery rate, and target margin can be adjusted in the workbook as the product and sales process mature.
