## Objection Responses

### "We already use SAM.gov."

That makes sense. GovCaptureAI is not trying to replace SAM.gov. The point is what happens after the opportunity is found: fit scoring, bid/no-bid rationale, workflow status, and proposal draft readiness.

### "We are not ready for AI proposal writing."

That is fair. The first value may be qualification and decision support. Proposal drafting can stay as a reviewed first outline, not final content.

### "Our company data is messy."

That is common. The Discovery Sprint is designed for that situation: organize enough company profile, capability, and target-market context to run a useful pilot.

### "We need CRM integration."

Eventually, yes. For the early pilot, the question is whether the capture workflow itself improves opportunity screening and proposal readiness before we add integrations.
